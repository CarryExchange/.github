# Carry

Benchmarks and markets for the price of intelligence.

Carry publishes independent benchmarks for the price of AI inference and is building markets for AI inference, compute and the infrastructure behind them.

- **Carry Indices** publishes CICI, the Carry Inference Cost Index, and CRTI, the Carry Realized Token Index, every day.
- **Carry Exchange** is a venue for perpetual futures, tokenized spot, prediction markets and options on AI inference, AI compute and AI infrastructure assets.
